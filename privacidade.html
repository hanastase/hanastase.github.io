CORE-TKTK — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

1. What we access. With the creator's permission, the app reads the basic profile information TikTok provides to the app (open ID, display name, avatar) and uses the permission to upload a video as a draft.

2. What we store. The access token and the creator's videos stay on the creator's own computer. The app has no server and does not send this data to the developer or to any third party, except to TikTok itself when uploading the draft.

3. What we do not do. We do not sell data, do not show ads, do not collect data from other TikTok users and do not read the creator's private messages or videos.

4. Retention and deletion. Data stays on the computer until the creator deletes it. Removing the app and its folder deletes everything. The creator can revoke access in the TikTok app settings.

5. Children. The app is not intended for people under 18.
